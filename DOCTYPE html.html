About Me

Hey! I am Driss, a Moroccan guy who grew up in Tunisia for 17 years.

I am a motivated person with experience in volunteering and helping the community. I want to use my skills in teamwork and problem solving to work in a great organization. I'm excited to make a positive difference and grow while doing meaningful work.

While living in Tunisia, I led and took part in many activities. I was part of groups that support migrants, with or without papers. I also joined environmental projects—things like saving water and keeping nature clean.

Now I am ready for new adventures—maybe in a new country! I want to grow, explore, and turn my dreams into reality.
